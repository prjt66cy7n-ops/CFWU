# Draft artwork gallery

These are generated artwork sources for **Your Farm With Unicorns**, not finished animation atlases. The gameplay prototype bundles portraits, rider and tack palettes, care/coop props, scenery props, magic props and farmhouse furniture. Remaining sheets are production references only; none are finished animation atlases. No further art approval is required; continue processing and production in the agreed visual style.

The original care-and-tack sheet was approved as the style direction. All new PNGs have alpha transparency, confirmed by reading their channels. Some preview tools display colors stored under fully transparent pixels; those colors are not visible during correct alpha compositing.

The sheets were generated from written art descriptions. They do not reconstruct the exact uploaded concept pixels. Approved maps and scene layouts remain the visual references; modular scenery pieces do not replace them.

## Production work still needed

- Separate objects with per-object bounds; do not blindly crop every sheet into equal cells. Several silhouettes cross nominal grid boundaries or touch outer edges.
- Clean edge artifacts, normalize scale and anchors, and check small-screen readability.
- Roo's directional sheet contains eight standing references, not gait or jump animation. Check all facings, body proportions, diamond marking and anatomical sock placement before runtime use.
- Riders are standing outfit previews, not mounted rider poses. Tack artwork is inventory imagery, not aligned equipment overlays.
- Wing poses need consistent roots and silhouette alignment; they are not a tested flap cycle.
- Horse portraits are a provisional set of 13, not an approved final named roster. The uploaded lineup had 14; this portrait draft uses Roo plus 12 varied coats and omits the second generic bay entry. Final names and selection data are not changed by this art batch.
- Add other horses' full-body poses, movement/jump frames, white unicorn bodies and palette variants, mounted rider poses, approved scene layers and audio.
